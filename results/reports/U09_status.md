**Finding.**
- On strong synthetic φ regimes, R2 (MS-AR(1) on d*) beats the rolling-φ baseline: Youden J 0.63–0.67 against
  0.30–0.53. Without regimes it invents them: on a constant-φ series it calls "fast" 40 % of the time, with 78 %
  degenerate refits.
- R1 (HMM) does not beat trailing σ: J 0.58 / 0.42 against 0.58 / 0.51.
- R3 (BOCPD) is conservative: it catches 21 % of σ doublings within 21 sessions, with almost no false alarms.
