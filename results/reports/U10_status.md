2. **The MDE column used the window's σ, not the registered σ.** It now uses the registered σ_d, and the report

   record now carries `data_hash_diag`.
4. **The spec's estimator windows were descriptive only.** They are now checked against `REGISTERED` (test added).
5. **Gate rules and columns were validated after the look.** They are now validated before it (test added).
6. **The Δμ statistic and the gated stream use slightly different lags** at gate transitions: the overnight leg is
   decided at t − 2. This is a lag, not leakage. A note was added to report §3.

The reviewer also confirmed:
- **Engine legs.** Row t = f_{t−2} on the overnight leg + f_{t−1} on the intraday leg. So gate t − 1 is the tightest
  causal alignment, and gate t would leak.
- **No look-ahead.** R1 / R2 use one-step-ahead probabilities, and the favourable sets use trailing quantiles only.
- **Real-input pre-flight** (read only, no P&L): 0 NaN in any state, regime or gate on 2016–2025. Every gate is on
  and off. The H1 stream check passes.
